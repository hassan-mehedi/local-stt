Welcome to local-stt

Speech to text that runs on this Mac. Nothing you say leaves it.

  • Dictation. Press a shortcut, speak, press it again. The text appears in the app you're typing in.
  • Meetings. Records you and the other side as separate tracks, then writes a transcript.
  • Files. Transcribe audio and video files to text or subtitles.

Setup takes about 5 minutes plus the model download: permissions, a speech model, your shortcut and meetings.

Permissions

macOS asks you to allow each of these once.

Allowed something and it still says missing? Quit stt and start it again.

Speech model

The model turns your voice into text. It runs offline once downloaded.

Meetings

Start and stop a meeting from the menu bar. The transcript lands in this folder.

  • Use headphones. On speakers the other side leaks into your mic and shows up twice.
  • Your voice is "Me", everything the Mac plays is "Them".
  • English transcripts take a few minutes per hour of meeting. Bengali runs on the CPU and takes about 30 minutes per hour of audio.

You're set

local-stt lives in the menu bar: the mic icon at the top right of your screen.

  • Record a meeting: click the mic icon, then Record meeting.
  • Change anything later from the menu: Settings…, or Setup guide… to run this again.

All done
